# Question performance and marks exports

Open a classroom's **Planning** tab. Create a **Marks template**, choose the columns, rename/reorder their headings, and choose released or approved scores. Registration number and result status are required. Save and explicitly approve the template before using it.

In **Marks exports**, choose an assessment and approved template, then prepare a preview. Download CSV or Excel from that preview. Saved previews preserve the exact template version, headings, score basis, marks and provenance; later marks or template edits do not rewrite them. Prepare another preview when new results are needed. Downloads are audited and restricted to classroom staff. Spreadsheet formula-like text is escaped, while numeric Excel marks remain numbers.

Approved exports use reviewed answers and approved marks from the current rubric. Released exports preserve the latest released result; `released_previous` identifies a release awaiting replacement. Missing/unapproved marks remain blank, never zero. Withdrawn students can be included explicitly with blank scores.

Open an approved question bank or paper preview to link a question to a specific assessment/rubric question. Links require a reason and retain history when removed. The performance panel shows reviewed, approved current-rubric counts and averages for each explicit link. Stale document/source/rubric versions suppress those statistics. Questions with different maxima are not pooled, and the app does not infer equivalence between questions. Existing source snapshots show explicit document reuse.

The panels use the shared black/blue and light themes, concise labels, accessible info tips, finite motion and reduced-motion support. No AI service is needed for these tools.
